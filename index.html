About me

A PM who leads with curiosity

I'm a product manager with a passion for building SaaS products that make complex things feel simple. My work starts with people — their frustrations, their workarounds, the things they don't say out loud in interviews but reveal through their behavior.

I've worked across the full product lifecycle, from early discovery and shaping in 0-to-1 environments to scaling established platforms. I'm comfortable in ambiguity, love a well-structured problem, and believe that the best roadmap decision is one your whole team can rally behind.

Outside of product, I'm drawn to behavioral economics, reading about urban design, and cooking elaborate meals that almost always work out.

My approach

How I think about product

A few principles that shape how I work — born from experience, failure, and reading too many post-mortems.

🔍

The real problem is rarely the stated one

Stakeholders come with solutions disguised as problems. I spend the first week of any project questioning the framing before even thinking about answers.

🤝

Alignment is a product, too

The best roadmap that no one believes in will fail. I invest heavily in making sure my team understands the why before the what.

📐

Constraints are creative fuel

I've shipped more impactful things under tight timelines and limited resources than in open-ended explorations. Constraints force prioritization and clarity.

🌊

Slow down to go fast

Two extra days of discovery can save six weeks of building the wrong thing. I protect this space even when it's uncomfortable to do so.

💬

Users lie, behaviors don't

I trust what I see in session recordings and analytics more than what people tell me in interviews. I listen for the gap between the two.

✂️

Simplicity is a decision, not a default

Simple products are harder to build than complex ones. Every piece of abstracted complexity is a deliberate, defended choice.
